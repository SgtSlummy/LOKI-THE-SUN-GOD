## Carry-Forward Notes

- Hierarchy is usually built through size, contrast, placement, and spacing.
- Good font pairing depends more on role separation than on using many typefaces.
- A limited typographic scale keeps a layout calm and readable.
- White space is active structure, not leftover emptiness.
- Accessibility checks matter even in beginner poster work, especially readable lines, contrast, and comfortable spacing.
- A strong beginner assignment should have one main decision to solve, not many unrelated decisions.
