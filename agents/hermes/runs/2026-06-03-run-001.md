## Skill cards

### Mythos Provider Gate

- Rule: Check provider-key presence without printing secrets before `mythos chat` or `mythos run`.
- How Hermes applies it: record present/absent only, then choose model-free concepts when absent.
- Why it matters: prevents accidental secret disclosure and avoids fake live-run claims.

### Parallel Teacher Isolation

- Rule: Dispatch teacher agents only when scopes are read-only, independent, and non-overlapping.
- How Hermes applies it: one teacher per Council domain; no shared write targets.
- Why it matters: parallelism improves coverage without corrupting artifacts.

### Provenance Before Memory

- Rule: Retain only lessons with operational value, evidence, safety boundary, and cheap future verification.
- How Hermes applies it: every memory record includes source, direct/inferred status, and verification path.
- Why it matters: durable memory should be auditable and reversible.

### Status Trust Boundary

- Rule: Separate agent-authored claims from verified runtime state.
- How Hermes applies it: future reports should mark accepted, staged, running, verified, failed, and stale states distinctly.
- Why it matters: avoids automation bias and over-trust in generated status.

### Orchestration With Guardrails

- Rule: Handoffs, tools, swarms, sessions, and traces increase capability but do not reduce approval requirements.
- How Hermes applies it: use orchestration patterns only behind explicit authority checks and eval/rollback notes.
- Why it matters: multi-agent systems multiply both throughput and blast radius.
